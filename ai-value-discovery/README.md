# AI Value Discovery Kit — Selling Agentic AI to a Fintech

A pre-sales kit that takes a fintech prospect from **"what could AI do for us?"** to a **fixed-price, outcome-based pilot** that is built to go into production. A successful pilot also positions the partner well for any later RFP to choose an implementation partner.

## Customer-stated challenges (from early conversations)

1. **Knowledge silos.** Acquisitions have left too many apps; knowledge is hard to share between team members and across teams.
2. **Different tech stacks.** Maintaining several stacks is a challenge.

These are addressed through two **anchor use cases** (X1 Enterprise Knowledge & Expertise Agent, X2 Multi-Stack Maintenance & Modernisation Agent), which appear in every step below.

## Documents

| Step | Document | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Top Agentic AI Use Cases](01-agentic-ai-use-cases.md) | Top 10 core fintech use cases, top 10 horizontal-function use cases (F&A, HR, IT, Legal, ...), 2 anchor use cases for the stated challenges, and common Non-Functional Requirements |
| 2 | [Discovery Questionnaire](02-discovery-questionnaire.md) | Per-BU probing questions with hidden "🔎 signals" and a scoring rubric to find pilot-worthy use cases |
| 3 | [3-Day AI Value Discovery Workshop](03-ai-value-discovery-workshop.md) | Day 1: 6-hr Business AI Orientation + 2-hr senior-leader alignment. Days 2–3: 60-min BU discovery sessions + daily 1-hr deep-dive buffer + read-out |
| 4 | [Outcome-Based Pilot Proposal](04-outcome-based-pilot-proposal.md) | 6 use cases, ≥3 guaranteed to demonstrate 30–40% cost-savings potential over 8–12 months; fixed price with 30% at risk; assumptions; RFP positioning |
| — | [AI Implementation Accelerators](05-ai-implementation-accelerators.md) | Production-grade platform and use-case packs that cut pilot effort by ~50% and avoid rebuilding for production |

## Flow

```
Use-case catalogue ──► Questionnaire (sent to each BU) ──► 3-day workshop ──► Pilot portfolio (5–6)
        ▲                                                                            │
        └──────── Accelerators (reduce cost ~50%, production-grade) ◄────────────────┘
                                                                                     ▼
                                     Outcome-based fixed-price pilot ──► Production / RFP advantage
```

> All value ranges and prices are **illustrative** and must be validated against the client's baselines during discovery.
